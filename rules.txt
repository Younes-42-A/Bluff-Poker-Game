==================================================
          BLUFF POKER / GAME RULES & CODE ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

[1] GAME RULES & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------
- Objective: Maintain your chip stack while draining the opponent's chips, or finish with the most chips after 12 rounds.
- Starting balance: Each player begins with 100 chips.
- Round flow:
  1. Draw: The attacker draws two random cards (from 1 to 10) and calculates their actual sum (from 2 to 20).
  2. Bluff: The attacker claims a sum between 2 and 20. The claim may match the actual sum or be a lie.
  3. Defend: The defender chooses one of the following actions:
     - [1] Call bluff: Challenge the attacker's honesty without guessing the exact sum.
       * If the attacker lied (actual sum != claimed sum), the defender gains 10 chips and the attacker loses 10 chips.
       * If the attacker was honest (actual sum == claimed sum), the defender loses 10 chips and the attacker gains 10 chips.
     - [2] Pass / fold: Accept the claim and avoid challenging it.
       * The defender loses 10 chips, which are transferred to the attacker.
     - [3] Raise: Increase the stake to 25 chips.
       * If the attacker lied, the defender gains 25 chips and the attacker loses 25 chips.
       * If the attacker was honest, the defender loses 25 chips and the attacker gains 25 chips.
- Game-over conditions:
  * Either player's chip count reaches zero.
  * Twelve rounds are completed; the player with the most remaining chips wins.

[2] CODE ARCHITECTURE & MODULES
--------------------------------------------------
The project uses object-oriented programming and is divided into three main game modules:

1. player.py (Player class)
   - Represents the human player.
   - Manages the player's name, chip balance, drawn cards, and actual card sum.
   - Provides draw_cards(), gain_chips(), lose_chips(), and make_bluff() methods.

2. bluff_poker_AI.py (AIPlayer class)
   - Represents the AI opponent.
   - make_defense_decision() evaluates the opponent's claim using the probability distribution of card sums and the remaining chip balance.
   - make_bluff() generates calculated claims, including strategic bluffs and truthful claims.

3. BluffGame.py (BluffGame class)
   - Implements the main game engine and turn flow.
   - Switches attacker and defender roles using current_player_index.
   - resolve_round() compares the actual sum with the claim and applies the outcome for Call, Pass, or Raise.
   - check_game_over() checks the ending conditions and declares the winner.

[3] LOSS WARNING
--------------------------------------------------
When the human player loses, the GUI side panel displays a fictional system-wipe warning for dramatic effect. This is purely visual: the game does not execute operating-system commands, delete files, or modify the operating system.
